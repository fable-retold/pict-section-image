# Pict Section Image

> Responsive image viewer section for Pict — fit, zoom, pan, fullscreen.

- **Fit by default** — the whole image fits the stage on phones, tablets, desktops and projected displays
- **Zoom & pan** — zoom is a multiplier of the fit size; above 1× the stage scrolls (touch- and mouse-friendly)
- **Fullscreen** — fill the browser viewport and back; `Esc` exits
- **Click-to-zoom, download, checkerboard** — a complete toolbar, all config-driven
- **Self-contained** — one `pict-view`, built-once imperative `<img>`; multiple viewers coexist on a page
- **Embeddable** — standalone, or the ordinary-image lens inside `pict-section-fileview`
